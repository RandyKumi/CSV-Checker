🛡️ Privacy & Security Guarantee

Your data is 100% secure because it never leaves your device.

Unlike typical web applications that upload your CSV to a remote server for processing, Datacheck runs an advanced data engine directly inside your web browser.

How to prove it yourself:

  1. The Airplane Mode Test: Load this website, turn off your Wi-Fi, and upload a file. The app will work perfectly offline.
  2. The Network Test: Open your browser's Developer Tools (F12), click the Network tab, and upload a file. You will see that absolutely zero data is sent across the internet.
  3. Open Source: The code that runs this application is completely transparent and running right in your browser.
